About Cloud Learn & Media

One company with a simple belief: useful digital products, made carefully, can change everyday life for students, readers, and job seekers across India.

Our story

It began in the classroom

Cloud Learn & Media began in the classroom. Our founder, a Physics professor, spent years teaching students preparing for JEE, NEET, and board examinations — and saw the same problem again and again: quality guidance was either too expensive or buried under noise.

Cloud Pathshala was born to fix that — structured, affordable preparation built around how students actually learn. From there, the same philosophy grew into new areas: The Cloud Pulse for readers who want news without the clutter, and The Cloud Career for job seekers who need every opportunity in one trusted place.

Today, Cloud Learn & Media is the home of this growing family — independent platforms sharing one standard of quality.
