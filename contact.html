Frequently asked questions

How do I join a course on Cloud Pathshala?

Visit cplearn.in, browse the courses, and enroll directly online. If you have questions before enrolling, email us with "Cloud Pathshala" as the topic.

Are the job listings on The Cloud Career verified?

Listings are aggregated from official government, bank, and institutional sources and refreshed continuously. Always apply through the official link provided with each listing.

Yes — we're open to partnerships, advertising, and collaboration. Email contact@cloudlm.in with "Partnership / Business" as the topic and a short description of your proposal.

Do you hire teachers or content creators?

We're always interested in hearing from talented educators. Send a short introduction and your subject expertise to our email, and we'll get back to you if there's a fit.
